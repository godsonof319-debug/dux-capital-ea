How it works

Three filters. One decision.

The EA doesn't fire on a single signal — it stacks three layers before every entry.

01

EMA Trend Filter

Fast and slow moving averages set direction bias first. No trades against the prevailing trend.

02

RSI Momentum Trigger

Once direction is confirmed, RSI pinpoints overbought/oversold pressure to time the entry.

03

ADX Strength Gate

ADX filters out flat, choppy conditions — trades fire only when the market has real direction.
